\## The engineer behind this blog



I've spent years in the trenches of production infrastructure — 3am incidents, 

cascading failures, Kubernetes clusters that decided to have opinions at the 

worst possible time.



I'm Ramya Kolli, a Senior DevOps Engineer based in Bangalore. I've built and 

broken enough infrastructure to know what actually matters versus what looks 

good in an architecture diagram.



\## Why I started this



Every junior engineer I've worked with hits the same walls. Not because they 

aren't smart — they are. But because most DevOps content online is either too 

shallow to be useful, written by someone who read the docs but never ran this 

in production, or designed to sell you a course rather than solve your problem.



This blog is different. Everything here comes from real production experience.
